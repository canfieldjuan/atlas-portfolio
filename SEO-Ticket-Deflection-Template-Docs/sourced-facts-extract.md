# Sourced Facts Extract — Support Ticket Deflection

Extracted from three research briefs (2026-05-27). Every fact below is
attributed to a named source. Ranked from most to least impactful to
business bottom line and employee wellbeing.

Legend:
- **[M]** = directly measured (survey, benchmark, operating data)
- **[E]** = estimated/modeled (directionally robust, flagged)
- **[V]** = vendor-reported case study (inherently selection-biased)

---

## TIER 1 — Direct bottom-line cost (hard numbers, CFO-visible)

### 1. The cost gap between assisted and self-service

- **7.3x multiplier:** Assisted human contacts average $13.50 vs. $1.84 for
  self-service. [M] — Gartner, "Benchmarks to Assess Your Customer Service
  Costs," J.J. Moncus, February 1, 2024.
- **HDI 2024 benchmark:** Live agent interactions average $22.00 vs. $2.93
  for simple self-service resolutions. [M] — HDI, 2024 Support Center
  Practices report.
- **80–100x across full resolution journey** when channel-switching is
  included. [M] — Gartner / Devin Poole, "Rethink Your Customer Service
  Strategy to Drive Self-Service," 2019 (study of 8,000+ customer journeys).
- **B2B live interaction:** $13+ per contact. B2C: $7+. [M] — HBR, "Kick-Ass
  Customer Service," Dixon, Ponomareff, Turner, DeLisi, Jan–Feb 2017.
- **SaaS/tech human-handled ticket:** $25–$35 average; enterprise B2B:
  $30–$60. [E] — Industry synthesis via Lorikeet 2026 benchmarks.
- **AI chatbot resolution:** $0.50–$1.00 per resolution. [E] — Industry
  synthesis.
- **Human email ticket:** $6.29–$16.13 average. [E] — Industry synthesis.
- **Human phone ticket:** $17–$25 average. [E] — Industry synthesis.
- **MetricNet IT service desk:** Average cost per ticket $15.56, ranging from
  $2.93 to $46.69. Cost per minute of voice handling: $2.13 (2018). [M] —
  MetricNet benchmarking, cited via HDI and BMC.
- **Median customer service spend:** 0.7% of company revenue. [M] — Gartner,
  "Benchmarks to Assess Your Customer Service Costs," February 2024.
- **Full channel cost table** (low-end to high-end, industry synthesis):
  Self-service portal: $0.10–$4.00; AI chatbot: $0.50–$3.00; Human email:
  $6.29–$16.13; Human live chat: $10.00–$16.00; Human phone: $17.00–$25.00;
  SaaS/tech: $18.00–$35.00; Enterprise B2B: $30.00–$60.00. [E] — Industry
  synthesis.

### 2. The repeat-contact multiplier

- **30% of all contact center interactions are repeat contacts** on the same
  issue. The average customer issue requires 2.3 contacts to resolve. [E] —
  Lorikeet 2026 synthesis.
- **40–60% of inbound support volume is highly repetitive queries**; rising to
  70% in high-growth B2C environments. [E] — Industry synthesis.
- **Gartner: 74% of customer issues reaching live agents could have been
  resolved through self-service** if the relevant knowledge article existed or
  was accessible. [M] — Gartner study, cited in CoSupport AI analysis.
- **CoSupport AI: each unresolved ticket generates 2.4 follow-up messages** on
  average. [V] — CoSupport AI research.
- **Each additional call required to resolve a single issue drops CSAT by
  16%.** [M] — SQM Group, multi-year benchmark.
- **A 1% improvement in FCR reduces operating costs by 1%** while increasing
  CSAT by the same amount. [M] — SQM Group.
- **22% of repeat calls involve downstream issues** related to the original
  problem. [M] — CEB / Effortless Experience, Dixon, Toman, DeLisi.

### 3. Agent attrition cost (the multiplier no one budgets for)

- **Annual contact center turnover: 31.2%** (2024), up from 28.1% in 2023.
  [M] — Metrigy 2024 research.
- **Insignia Resources: 40–45% annual turnover** average; high-stress sectors
  reach 55–61%. [M] — Insignia Resources.
- **Replacement cost: $10,000–$20,000 per agent** in direct recruiting,
  training, and productivity-gap expenses. [M] — Metrigy 2024; [E] —
  McKinsey.
- **SHRM: replacement cost at 50–75% of annual salary.** [M] — SHRM /
  Society of Workforce Planning Professionals.
- **TalentKeepers: $8,780 average replacement cost.** [M] — TalentKeepers.
- **Total cumulative impact of losing one agent: $22,500–$46,000** when
  productivity loss, morale degradation, and knowledge loss are included.
  [E] — Insignia Resources, industry synthesis.
- **New hire ramp: 6–8 months to reach full performance.** [E] — Insignia
  Resources.
- **For a 100-agent center at 31% turnover: $700,000+/year** in replacement
  costs. [M] — Metrigy calculation.
- **For a 100-agent center at industry-average turnover: $2.25M–$4.6M/year**
  total attrition cost. [E] — Insignia Resources.
- **SQM Group benchmark: 38% agent turnover** is the industry average. 47% of
  call center managers cite high turnover and absenteeism as their #1
  operating problem. [M] — SQM Group 2023 Benchmark Report.
- **First-year attrition: 69–73%.** [M] — Insignia Resources.
- **$10M annual replacement cost for a 1,000-agent team at 40% turnover.**
  [E] — Frost & Sullivan, cited in Intradiem.
- **Frost & Sullivan: up to $35,000 to replace one agent** including
  advertising, interviewing, onboarding, initial training. [E] — Frost &
  Sullivan, cited in Intradiem.

### 4. Salesforce 2024–2025 agent-time data

- **Agents spend only 39% of their time servicing customers.** The rest goes
  to internal meetings, admin tasks, and manually logging case notes. [M] —
  Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition, April 2024 (n=5,500+ service
  professionals, 30 countries).
- **Reps using AI spend 20% less time on routine cases — freeing ~4 hours per
  week.** [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 7th Edition, 2025 (n=6,500).
- **77% of agents report increased and more complex workloads** vs. a year
  prior. [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition.
- **Service teams estimate 30% of cases are currently handled by AI.**
  Projected to reach 50% by 2027. [E] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 7th
  Edition, 2025 (self-reported estimate by surveyed teams, not corporate
  forecast).

### 5. The context-switching tax (hidden in every ticket)

- **90 seconds typing a macro reply, 10 minutes of context assembly**
  (toggling between CRM, billing, ticketing tools to pull customer history).
  [E] — Industry synthesis, Friction Multiplier.
- **53% of agents at underperforming orgs toggle between multiple screens.**
  36% at high performers. [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition.

### 6. Password reset cost (the canonical example)

- **Single manual password reset: ~$70** fully loaded (labor, infrastructure,
  lost user productivity). [E] — Forrester Research, cited in NetGain /
  FastPassCorp.
- **20–50% of all IT help desk tickets are password-related.** [M] — Gartner /
  HDI 2024.
- **Self-service password reset typically deflects 60–80%** of these tickets.
  [V] — Vendor case studies.
- **Large enterprises can spend $5M+/year** on password resets alone. [E] —
  Forrester estimate.

## TIER 2 — Self-service failure and the leadership blindspot

### 7. The 14% problem

- **Only 14% of customer issues are fully resolved in self-service.** [M] —
  Gartner, August 19, 2024 press release (survey of 5,728 customers,
  fieldwork December 2023). Eric Keller, Senior Director, Gartner: "While
  73% of customers use self-service at some point in their customer service
  journey, it's concerning to see that so few fully resolve there."
- **73% of customers attempt self-service** at some point. [M] — Same source.
- **43% of self-service failures:** customers couldn't find content relevant
  to their issue. [M] — Same source.
- **45% of self-service failures:** customers said the company didn't
  understand what they were trying to do. [M] — Same source.
- **58% of consumers are unable to resolve issues on the web.** [M] — Nuance
  Communications, December 16, 2013.
- **59% of consumers are frustrated** they have to reach a live agent after
  self-service fails. [M] — Same source.

### 8. The leadership perception gap

- **Service leaders report 20–40% of live volume could be resolved in
  self-service.** [M] — Gartner / Devin Poole, "Delivering on the Digital
  Promise," 2019 (leader survey).
- **56% of service leaders are adding new channels rather than fixing existing
  ones.** [M] — Same source.
- **60% of service agents fail to promote self-service** in customer
  interactions. 12% make explicitly negative remarks about self-service. [M] —
  Gartner, June 2, 2025 press release (Keith McIntosh, survey of 5,801
  customers, Jan–Feb 2025).
- **Agent endorsement doubles customer adoption** next time. [M] — Same
  source.
- **81% of consumers want more self-service options**; leaders estimate demand
  at only ~60%. 21-point gap. [M] — EasyVista survey; 2024 CX Network
  consumer research.
- **Businesses underestimate poor customer experiences by 38% on average.**
  [M] — Khoros / Forrester Consulting, 2024 (n=200+ enterprise brands, 1,000+
  customers).
- **61% of service teams believe they're proactive** — only 33% of customers
  agree. 28-point gap. [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition.
- **20% of agents handle 80% of tickets** due to poor triage — accelerating
  burnout among top performers. [E] — Industry synthesis, Friction
  Multiplier.

### 8. FCR (First Contact Resolution) — the metric that lies

- **Industry-average FCR: 69%** (ranges from 43% to 88% across industries).
  [M] — SQM Group, 2024 FCR Benchmark Report (n=500+ North American call
  centers).
- **Companies measure FCR at ~76%; customers report it at ~40%** because of
  unforeseen downstream problems. [M] — The Effortless Experience, Dixon,
  Toman, DeLisi.
- **Organizations with speech analytics: 76% FCR.** Without: 23%. [M] —
  Aberdeen Group.

## TIER 3 — Agent burnout, morale, and time waste

### 9. Repetition as the burnout driver

- **90% of IT support professionals** report repetitive manual tasks
  contribute to low team morale. [M] — Insignia Resources.
- **74% of agents experience ongoing burnout.** [M] — Insignia Resources.
- **69% of service decision-makers** say agent attrition is a major or
  moderate challenge. [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition.
- **Over half of agents report burnout** whether in contact centers or field.
  [M] — Same source.

### 10. Hours lost to repetition

- **Customer support agents: 5 hours/week on repetitive tickets** (copy-paste
  order statuses, return policies). [M] — Gorgias research.
- **IT service desk: 30–40% of working hours on password resets and basic
  troubleshooting.** [M] — Flairstech research.
- **58% of organizations:** IT teams lose 5+ hours/week on repetitive
  business requests. [M] — Industry survey cited in Friction Multiplier.
- **Subject matter experts: 6–8 hours/week** answering duplicate inquiries.
  [M] — Same source.
- **Average employee: 8–12 hours/week** searching for information /
  re-establishing context. [E] — McKinsey, "The Social Economy," 2012
  (modeled, knowledge workers broadly, not support-specific).

### 11. Agent morale from NICE research (verbatim language)

- "Answering the same question over and over again isn't very rewarding and
  handling back-to-back customer complaints all day takes its toll." [M] —
  NICE corporate blog, "5 Ways to Prevent Contact Center Agent Burnout."
- "A day in the life of a contact center agent is often filled with
  mind-numbing repetition." [M] — Same source.
- **Industry attrition: 30–45% annually** (QATC). Average agent tenure: 3
  years. [M] — QATC / NICE.

### 12. Deloitte survey on agent overwhelm

- **3 out of 4 respondents:** agents overwhelmed by too many systems and too
  much information, causing longer calls and weaker outcomes. [M] — Deloitte
  2024 Global Contact Center Survey.
- **60% of agents:** training provides no value. [M] — Same source.
- **53% of agents at underperforming orgs toggle between multiple screens.**
  36% at high performers. [M] — Salesforce, "State of Service" 6th Edition.

## TIER 4 — Customer-side impact (churn, effort, loyalty)

### 13. The Effortless Experience canon

- **94% of customers with low-effort interactions intend to repurchase.** Only
  4% of high-effort customers report the same intent. [M] — CEB / Gartner,
  The Effortless Experience (Dixon, Toman, DeLisi, 2013), analysis of 97,000+
  customers.
- **81% of high-effort customers intend to spread negative word of mouth.**
  [M] — Same source.
- **96% of high-effort customers become actively disloyal** vs. 9% of
  low-effort customers. [M] — Original CEB research.
- **A low-effort interaction costs 37% less to resolve.** [M] — Same source.

### 14. The churn connection

- **32% of customers stop doing business with a brand after one negative
  experience** — even one they previously loved. [M] — PwC, Future of
  Customer Experience Survey.
- **~40% of customers defect** in any given year because FCR did not occur.
  [M] — SQM Group.
- **FCR below 50% increases quarterly churn by 15–20%** in competitive
  environments (SaaS, e-commerce). [E] — Industry synthesis.
- **US CX at lowest point since 2016.** Forrester 2024 US CX Index: average
  score hit all-time low of 69.3 (third consecutive year of decline). [M] —
  Forrester, June 17, 2024 (n=98,000+ US customers, 223 brands).
- Rick Parrish, Forrester VP: "US consumers are having, on average, the worst
  experiences in a decade." [M] — Same source.

### 15. Zendesk CX Trends 2026

- **74% of customers are frustrated when they have to repeat information.**
  [M] — Zendesk CX Trends 2026, November 18, 2025 (n=11,000+ respondents, 22
  countries).
- **85% of CX leaders say customers will drop brands** over unresolved issues,
  even on first contact. [M] — Same source.

### 16. Forrester 2025 Global CX Index

- **25% of US brands' CX rankings declined** in 2025, vs. only 7% that
  improved. [M] — Forrester Global CX Index 2025, June 24, 2025 (n=275,000+
  customers, 469 brands, 12 industries, 13 countries).

## TIER 5 — Analogous proof (deflection case studies, AI results)

### 17. Klarna AI (the canonical story — and its walk-back)

- **February 2024 launch:** AI handled 2.3M conversations in first month,
  two-thirds of customer service chats. "Equivalent work of 700 full-time
  agents." Estimated $40M profit improvement in 2024. 25% drop in repeat
  inquiries. Resolution time: 11 min → under 2 min. CSAT on par with humans.
  [V] — Klarna press release, February 27, 2024.
- **May 2025 walk-back:** CEO Sebastian Siemiatkowski (Bloomberg): "As cost
  unfortunately seems to have been a too predominant evaluation factor when
  organizing this, what you end up having is lower quality. Really investing
  in the quality of the human support is the way of the future for us." [M] —
  Bloomberg, May 2025; reported by Fortune, May 9, 2025.
- **Lesson:** Deflection without quality is a different cost line. The
  walk-back is more useful as proof than the original claim.

### 18. Intercom Fin customer benchmarks

- **Median deflection: 35–45%** on well-documented KBs. Best cases: 50–70%+.
  [V] — Intercom customer benchmarks.
- **Anthropic:** 50.8% resolution rate, 96% conversation participation, 1,700+
  hours saved in first month. [V] — Intercom case study.
- **Fundrise:** 50%+ resolution at 3 months. Sharesies: 70% in 12 weeks. [V]
  — Intercom case studies.

### 19. Other deflection case studies

- **Amazon Q Business bot (RAG-first):** 70% reduction in Tier-1 repetitive
  tickets in 4 weeks. Median response time: minutes → 1.2 seconds. Cost:
  under $500/month. Reclaimed 12 hours/week/agent. [V] — AWS case study.
- **Avatier Identity self-service:** 83% drop in password-related tickets.
  $420,000/year reduction in help desk operating costs. [V] — Avatier case
  study.
- **CoSupport AI / Shelterluv:** Resolved 73% of live chats, 61% of emails.
  SLA compliance restored. Ticket backlog resolved within 3 months. Learning
  from 20,000 historical interactions. [V] — CoSupport AI case study.
- **Bell Canada:** Reduced "calls per event" by 16% by mapping downstream
  issue patterns and pre-emptively addressing them. [M] — CEB / Effortless
  Experience.
- **McKinsey client:** Redesigned IVR, increased call containment 2–5%,
  improved satisfaction 10–25%. [V] — McKinsey / Qualtrics analysis.
- **McKinsey broader finding:** Brands transitioning to digital customer
  service can reduce costs by 30% while increasing CSAT by 19%. [V] —
  McKinsey / Qualtrics.
- **Verizon:** 85% call deflection rate after implementing advanced
  self-service. [V] — Balto analysis (vendor-reported).
- **InflowCX / Uplift:** 40% containment/deflection rate in IVR queue via
  empathetic voice AI. [V] — InflowCX case study.
- **Apptega Systems:** 75% reduction in compliance timeline, 18% less
  overhead, 233% increase in internal bandwidth via continuous evidence
  collection and automated mapping. [V] — Apptega case study.
- **Knowledge base ROI model:** 10 agents × $50/hr loaded cost, 30 min/week
  saved each = $13,000/year saved. Platform cost $79/seat = $790/year. ROI:
  1,546%. [E] — Modeled example in Friction Multiplier.
- **Higher Logic estimate:** On accepted-answer community threads, ~1 in 5
  pageviews results in deflection; on general KB articles, ~1 in 20. [V] —
  Higher Logic (vendor research, flagged as estimate).

### 20. Peak-volume data (prospecting triggers)

- **Zendesk Benchmark Q4/2013 (retail):** Ticket volume rose 42% between Q3
  and Q4. Agents handled 19% more tickets on average between November and
  December. [M] — Zendesk Benchmark, Q4/2013 (16,000 participating companies;
  retail-specific, not current cross-industry).
- **Concentrix:** Peaks of 3–5x normal demand during Black Friday for retail.
  [V] — Concentrix.
- **Salesforce:** Helped customers field and resolve nearly 33.3 billion case
  interactions throughout the 2024 holidays. [V] — Salesforce press release,
  January 6, 2025.

### 21. Forward-looking projections (not accomplished outcomes)

- **Gartner (March 2025):** "By 2029, agentic AI will autonomously resolve 80%

  Director Analyst, Gartner, March 5, 2025. **Flagged as projection, not

- **Forrester (November 2025):** "While the vision of AI-first customer

- **Klarna headcount reduction:** ~22% reduction (to ~3,500) under AI-first

## TIER 6 — Scenario math (models to run in outreach, not publish as facts)

### 20. The repeat-cost formula

- $AnnualRepeatCost = TotalTickets × RepeatRate × AssistedCPC$. Example:
  200,000 tickets/year × 25% repeats × $13.50 = **$675,000/year** on
  questions already answered. [E] — Repeat-Question Playbook model.
- **15,000 monthly tickets via human agents: up to $330,000/month.** At
  deflection: ~$44,000/month. [E] — Friction Multiplier model.

### 21. The documentation-debt model

- Tickets-that-should-have-been-deflected = Top-10 ticket categories × annual
  volume × assisted-CPC. Almost always 7 figures. [E] — Ariglad / HDI usage.
- Intercom's own data: each product release requires 3–6 article updates. A
  significant product change: 5–8 hours of content work. [M] — Intercom
  operating guidance.

## CAVEATS (do not lose these)

- The 2019 "9% self-service resolution" and 2024 "14%" appear contradictory
  but come from different survey populations and question framings. The 14% is
  the current best number from Gartner. Use 14%.
- McKinsey's "1.8 hours/day searching" is from a 2012 broad knowledge-worker
  analysis, not support-specific. Flag as directional.
- Cost-per-ticket varies by 20x across industries. Gartner 2024 median ($1.84
  vs. $13.50) is the most current apples-to-apples comparison. Use it.
- The HDI $22.00 live-agent figure and Gartner $13.50 assisted figure differ
  because HDI measures IT service desks specifically; Gartner measures
  customer service broadly. Both are valid in their domain. Cite the Gartner
  figure for general CX; HDI for IT-specific.
- All vendor case studies (Klarna, Intercom, Avatier, AWS, CoSupport, Verizon,
  Apptega, InflowCX) are selection-biased. The companies that publish are the
  ones that worked.
- Klarna's "700 agents" / "$40M savings" numbers are now disputed by Klarna
  itself. Use the walk-back quote, not the original headline numbers.
- Forrester's "$70 per password reset" is modeled, not directly measured.
- Some industry figures (McKinsey, Frost & Sullivan) are widely cited but
  modeled. Triangulate, don't present as point estimates.
- Intercom Fin resolution-rate complaints (12% real resolve rate) surface in
  user forums and competitor comparison pages — real but often surfaced by
  competitors. Reference cautiously.
- The Zendesk Benchmark "42% Q3-to-Q4 volume rise" is retail-specific from
  2013. Treat as a directional retail anchor, not a current cross-industry
  benchmark.
- Higher Logic deflection-per-pageview ratios (1 in 5, 1 in 20) are vendor
  research. Cite as Higher Logic's estimate.
- Forward-looking AI projections (Gartner 2029, Salesforce 2027) are analyst
  forecasts, not accomplished outcomes. Use for strategic framing only.
- Brad Cleveland and Annette Franz quotes located via secondary sources
  (TechTarget, Intercom blog); verbatim wording is consistent across reprints
  but link to original interviews.
- Some hedged/generic statements in KB-failure literature (e.g., "knowledge
  bases fail when content goes stale") are widely repeated but rarely sourced
  to a specific named author with a date. Where unattributable, paraphrase
  rather than quote.
- Scott Tran (Support Driven founder) does not have a publicly attributable
  quote on repetitive-ticket pain. Do not fabricate one.
- The CES/Effortless Experience research was originally CEB work, acquired by
  Gartner in 2017. Citations to "CEB" and "Gartner" for this research stream
  refer to the same source.
