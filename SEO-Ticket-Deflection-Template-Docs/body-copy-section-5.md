# Body Copy — Section 5: The Offer

---

**What you do.**

Upload a CSV of your past support tickets. Export from Zendesk, Intercom, Help
Scout, or any help desk that gives you a CSV. The export takes under five
minutes.

---

**What you get. Within 24 hours.**

A ranked fix list containing four deliverables:

1. **Repeat question clusters.** The questions your customers keep asking — in
   their own words, not paraphrased — ranked by how often they reach support.

2. **Customer-term-to-doc-term gaps.** Every mismatch between what people
   search and what your help center says. Customers type "how do I cancel."
   Your doc says "Subscription Termination." Google cannot match them. The
   report flags every gap, shows both terms, and ranks them by frequency.

3. **Source tickets and verbatim customer quotes.** The evidence behind every
   recommendation. Click through. Read the original language. Verify the
   finding yourself.

4. **A drafted FAQ for each gap.** Assembled from the actual answers your
   support team already wrote across closed tickets — in the language that
   already resolved the issue for a real customer. Ready to review, edit, and
   publish.

---

**What happens next.**

You review each recommendation. You edit each FAQ. You publish what you
approve. You discard what you do not.

Pages go up in customer language. Google indexes them. The next customer who
searches the same question finds your page — not your support form.

---

**Who it is for.**

Support leads at 15-to-75-person B2B SaaS companies who already know repeat
tickets are a problem, run an exportable help desk, and control their help
center.

**Who it is not for.**

Enterprise procurement cycles. Teams without an exportable ticket history.
Anyone looking for a chatbot that answers tickets — this does not.
