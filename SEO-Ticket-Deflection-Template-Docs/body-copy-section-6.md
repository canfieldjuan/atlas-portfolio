# Body Copy — Section 6: Risk Reversal

---

**Nothing goes live without you.**

The system does not publish. It does not modify your help center. It does not
answer tickets. It delivers a ranked fix list with drafted FAQs.

You review every recommendation. You edit every FAQ. You approve what you want
to publish. You discard what you do not. You are the final author of every word
that ships.

---

**Every finding is verifiable.**

Every recommendation links back to a real ticket with a real customer quote.
If it is in the report, it is in your queue. Click through and confirm.

Nothing is generated from a model's best guess. Nothing is synthesized from
training data. The system extracts patterns from your tickets. It does not
invent answers.

---

**24 hours from CSV to fix list.**

No implementation consultant. No six-month rollout. No enterprise procurement
cycle. No per-resolution pricing. No budget volatility.

Upload a CSV. Receive your ranked fix list within 24 hours. Review. Publish.
That is the entire workflow.

---

**What you are not risking.**

No AI talking to your customers. No automated publishing. No content going
stale without your review. No conversations marked "assumed resolved." No
model hallucination reaching a customer.

The mechanism is extraction, not automation. It reads your ticket history,
finds the pattern, and hands you a prioritized fix list with drafted answers.
You decide what happens next.
