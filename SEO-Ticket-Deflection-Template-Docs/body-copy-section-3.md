# Body Copy — Section 3: The Mechanism

---

Support Ticket Deflection turns your support tickets into a ranked fix list for
your help center. Three steps. No implementation consultant. No six-month
rollout.

---

**Step 1: Upload a CSV of your past support tickets.**

Export from Zendesk, Intercom, Help Scout, or any help desk that gives you a
CSV. The export takes under five minutes. The file contains what your tickets
already contain — the subject lines, the body text, the customer language, the
agent responses, the resolution notes.

That is the only input. You do not configure anything. You do not map fields.
You do not train a model.

---

**Step 2: Within 24 hours, you receive a ranked fix list.**

Not a suggestion list. Not a brainstorming prompt. A list ordered by how often
each question reaches support — backed by the actual tickets and verbatim
customer quotes that produced each finding.

What is in the report:

**Repeat question clusters.** The questions your customers keep asking — in
their own words, not paraphrased, not summarized — ranked by how often they
reach support. You see the exact phrasing customers used when they could not
find the answer and opened a ticket instead.

**Customer-term-to-doc-term gaps.** Every mismatch between what people search
and what your help center says. Customers type "how do I cancel my account."
Your help center says "Subscription Termination Procedure." Google cannot match
them. The gap is a ticket. The report flags every mismatch, shows you both
terms, and ranks them by frequency.

**Source tickets and verbatim customer quotes.** Every recommendation links
back to the specific tickets that produced it. You can click through, read the
original customer language, and verify the finding yourself. Nothing is
synthesized from thin air.

**A drafted FAQ for each gap.** Each FAQ is assembled from the actual answers
your support team already wrote across closed tickets — in the language that
already resolved the issue for a real customer. The draft is ready to review,
edit, and publish. You are not inventing answers. You are publishing the
answers your team already proved.

---

**Step 3: You review and publish. Nothing goes live without you.**

You read each recommendation. You edit each FAQ. You approve what you want to
publish. You discard what you do not.

Pages go up using the exact language your customers already type into Google.
Google indexes them. The next customer who searches that question finds your
page — not your support form.

---

**Why this works beyond the help center.**

Pages written in the language people actually search get more visibility on
Google. This is the foundational principle of SEO. It requires no case study.
It is demonstrable by any buyer in thirty seconds with their own Google search.

Your ticket queue already contains that language. Every time a customer
searches your help center, fails to find the answer, and opens a ticket
instead, they are telling you: here is the question I needed answered. Here
are the words I used to search for it. Here is the gap between what I asked
and what you published.

Support Ticket Deflection reads that signal at scale. Extracts the language.
Ranks the gaps. Drafts the answers. You publish. Google deflects.

---

**What Support Ticket Deflection does not do.**

It does not answer tickets. It does not touch your help desk. It does not
publish anything without your review. It does not replace agents. It does not
require a consultant, a six-month rollout, or an enterprise procurement cycle.

The mechanism is extraction, not automation. It reads your ticket history,
finds the pattern, and hands you a prioritized fix list with drafted answers.
You decide what happens next.
