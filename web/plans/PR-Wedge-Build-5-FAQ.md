# Plan: Build slice 5 — wedge FAQ (+3 questions) + step-by-step mirror

Fifth slice of the wedge landing-copy build (hero #78, mechanism #80, Push #81,
who-it's-for #82). Adds three common pre-upload questions to the FAQ and brings
FAQ #1/#2 in line with the "step-by-step" wording the hero/mechanism now use.

## Why this slice exists

- The FAQ answers eight questions but skips three the operator hears most before
  someone uploads: **does this replace our help desk**, **we just updated our
  help center — do we still need this**, and **how many tickets should I export**.
- FAQ #1/#2 still say "self-service answer(s)"; the hero (#78) and mechanism
  (#80) now say "step-by-step." A light mirror keeps the FAQ consistent.
- The FAQ feeds the page's **FAQ JSON-LD** (`faqJsonLd` is generated from
  `pricingFaqs`), so the additions also extend the structured data search/LLM
  surfaces read.

## Scope (this PR)

Slice phase: Product polish

1. **Three new FAQ items** (`page.tsx` `pricingFaqs`): "Do you replace our help
   desk?", "We just updated our help center — do we still need this?", "How many
   tickets should I export?" — placed in sensible flow positions.
2. **Step-by-step mirror** (`page.tsx` `pricingFaqs` #1/#2): "self-service
   answer(s)" → "step-by-step self-service answer(s)" — a light touch, not a
   page-wide term sweep. (Keeps "self-service", the page's dominant term;
   answers match the FAQ's existing no-contraction voice.)

### Files touched

- `web/plans/PR-Wedge-Build-5-FAQ.md` — this plan doc (new)
- `web/src/app/systems/support-ticket-deflection/page.tsx` — pricingFaqs (+3 items, #1/#2 mirror)

## Mechanism

- Three `{ q, a }` objects are inserted into `pricingFaqs`; `faqJsonLd`
  (generated by `pricingFaqs.map`) picks them up automatically, so the FAQ
  schema goes from 8 to 11 entries with no extra wiring.
- The mirror swaps only the #1/#2 answer phrasing.

## Intentional

- **Answers are claims-clean:** no deflection %, no guaranteed outcome, no
  auto-publish. "Fewer repeat questions reach the desk" is directional (the
  page's whole premise), not a quantified promise; "the gap is usually wording"
  is hedged; "the snapshot will tell you / we'll say so" keeps the honest out.
- **Mirror is intentionally light** — FAQ #1/#2 only. The page still says
  "self-service answers" in other sections; that's fine copy, not a stale value,
  so sweeping every instance would be scope creep, not consistency.

## Deferred

- **`/partner` route** → later slice.
- **Term consistency (1 word):** the hero (#78) says "self-serve" while the rest
  of the page (and these FAQs) say "self-service" — a minor wording split worth a
  later one-word tidy; out of scope here (B5 adds FAQ copy, doesn't sweep terms).
