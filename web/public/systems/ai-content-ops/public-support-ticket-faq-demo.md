## FAQ Output Excerpts

### Credit Report Disputes

**Question:** How can I dispute credit report information that keeps reappearing or has not been corrected?

Consumers repeatedly described disputed credit report information that was not corrected, was verified without enough explanation, or returned after earlier disputes.

**Action steps:**

1. Collect the report section, account name, dates, and any letters or screenshots that show what is wrong.
2. File the dispute with each credit reporting company that shows the information, and keep the confirmation number.
3. Attach supporting records instead of relying only on a short written explanation.
4. Track the response date and compare the updated report against the exact item you disputed.

**When to contact support:** Contact support or the reporting company again if the same item returns, the response does not explain what was verified, or the correction appears on one report but not another.

**Sources:** CFPB #1885409, CFPB #1973120, CFPB #2209426, CFPB #2591415, CFPB #3138626

### Mortgage Servicing Issues

**Question:** What should I do when a mortgage servicer says I am behind or mishandles escrow, payments, or loss mitigation?

The repeated pattern was not one isolated payment question. Consumers described servicer records that did not match their own, escrow changes that were hard to reconcile, or loss-mitigation steps that stalled without clear status.

**Action steps:**

1. Download your payment history, escrow statements, notices, and any loss-mitigation letters before calling.
2. Ask the servicer to identify the exact month, fee, escrow line, or document causing the issue.
3. Send missing documents through a trackable channel and keep the upload or delivery confirmation.
4. Request a written explanation when the servicer says the account is delinquent or incomplete.

**When to contact support:** Contact support again if the servicer cannot point to the specific missing item, applies payments differently than your records show, or gives conflicting status updates across calls and letters.

**Sources:** CFPB #2326114, CFPB #2619048, CFPB #2885301, CFPB #3377106

### Debt Collection Disputes

**Question:** How do I respond when a collector contacts me about a debt I do not recognize or says I still owe?

Consumers often reported collection attempts for debts they did not recognize, debts they believed were already paid, or accounts where the collector had not provided enough validation detail.

**Action steps:**

1. Do not rely on a phone call alone. Ask for the collector name, account number, original creditor, amount, and written validation.
2. Compare the validation notice with your own records, credit reports, and payment history.
3. If the debt is not yours or the amount is wrong, send a written dispute and keep a copy.
4. Document every contact attempt, including dates, phone numbers, letters, and any payment demands.

**When to contact support:** Contact support, the collector, or the relevant regulator if collection continues without validation, the collector reports disputed debt as undisputed, or you receive threats or contact patterns that seem improper.

**Sources:** CFPB #1678934, CFPB #2047189, CFPB #2755042, CFPB #3446907
